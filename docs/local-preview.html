    Conversion equations

    Temperature to Saturated Pressure

    Use t in °C and T = t + 273.15 in K. psat is in kPa.

    K=4.39553−6.2442(T1000) +9.953(T1000)2 −5.151(T1000)3 psat=22100× 10K(1−647.30T)

    Pressure to Saturated Temperature

    α = ln(p), with absolute pressure p in kPa. The equation covers 0–93 °C.

    tsat=6.54+14.526α +0.7389α2 +0.0949α3 +0.4569p0.1984
