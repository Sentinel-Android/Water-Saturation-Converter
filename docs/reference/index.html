Reference

Definitions, units, calculation methods, and sources for the saturation converter.

Quantities and methods

Quantity or methodUnits or rangeMeaning
Temperature, t°C, °F, KThe input or calculated saturation temperature. The internal temperature unit is °C.
Absolute pressure, pkPa, Pa, bar, MPa, psiaSaturation pressure measured relative to a vacuum. The internal pressure unit is kPa.
MATLAB saturation table−60 to 160 °C; 0.00108 to 618.1392 kPaThe original tabulated data with piecewise cubic Hermite interpolation (PCHIP). As in the MATLAB code, values outside the table are extrapolated and marked in the result.
Textbook: temperature to pressure0 to 200 °CThe original temperature correlation, using T = t + 273.15 in kelvin and returning pressure in kPa.
Textbook: pressure to temperature0 to 93 °C output rangeThe direct logarithmic temperature correlation. Its accepted pressure range is shown by the calculator.

Equations

The conversion equations are shown below the main calculator. The two textbook correlations are separate approximations; the reverse calculation uses the original direct correlation.
